Per-machine calibration instead of one laptop's numbers. price::MINTED carries a row per (architecture, kernel) pair — arm64 macOS and native x86_64 Linux, both minted over one frozen 60 MiB corpus — and price::active() resolves the running target against it. A machine with no row declines every pattern with BuildError::Uncalibrated rather than inheriting foreign ratios, which matters because the two ISAs disagree about twofold in opposite directions: skip/walk 0.014 vs 0.026, sieve/walk 0.395 vs 0.196. Absolute speed provably cannot move a decision (scaling_the_whole_calibration_changes_no_decision).
